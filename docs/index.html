Route Story Studio · v0.1.0

Help and documentation

Your route stays on your device. Route Story Studio reads, analyses and exports GPX and KML files in your browser. It does not upload them.

Start here

  1. Open the studio. Use the live application or run the project locally.
  2. Load a route. Choose a GPX or KML file, drag one into the import area, or try the included sample.
  3. Shape the story. Choose a layout, units, colours and the details you want to show.
  4. Export. Download a scalable SVG or a standard/high-resolution PNG.

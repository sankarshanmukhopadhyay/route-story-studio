ROUTE STORY STUDIO v0.3.0

Help and guided journey

Route Story Studio is designed first for one straightforward job: turn an existing GPX or KML route into a polished poster.

  1. Import GPX or KML.
  2. Choose a colour, photograph or consent-based map background.
  3. Adjust layout, zoom, route styling and annotations.
  4. Export SVG, PNG or JPEG.

Advanced option

Google Maps link acquisition is retained under a collapsed experimental section. It requires external services and is not necessary for normal poster creation.

Route Story Studio · v0.2.0

Help and documentation

Your route stays on your device. GPX, KML, projects, photographs, annotations and exports are processed in the browser. Only map mode makes a third-party request, after explicit consent.

Start here

  1. Load a route. Choose GPX or KML, open a saved project, or use the sample.
  2. Shape the story. Add a title, choose a template and adjust route styling.
  3. Add context. Use a photograph, consent-based map or route-relative annotations.
  4. Save or export. Save locally, exchange a `.rssproj` file, or download SVG, PNG or JPEG.

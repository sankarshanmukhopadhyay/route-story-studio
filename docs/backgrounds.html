Route Story Studio · v0.2.0 development

Photo and map backgrounds

Choose a solid colour, a photograph processed locally, or a map loaded only after explicit consent.

Photographs

JPEG, PNG and WebP images are decoded and processed in your browser. Images are limited to 5 MB and 40 megapixels. Temporary object URLs are revoked after validation, and exported images are newly rendered rather than copying the original file metadata.

Map backgrounds

No map request is made until you select map mode and press Consent and load map. The request sends the approximate visible route area and ordinary network metadata to OpenStreetMap's tile service. At most nine tiles are fetched, with time and response-size limits.

Attribution: OpenStreetMap attribution remains visible in generated artefacts that use the map background.

Privacy choice

Use a solid colour or imported photograph to keep the complete workflow local. Map mode is optional and clearly separated from local-only processing.

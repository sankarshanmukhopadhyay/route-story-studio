Elevation metrics

Route Story Studio uses a few related elevation terms. Here is the simplest way to read them.

What the poster shows

The generated poster shows Elevation range. That is the highest point minus the lowest point represented in the route.

Total climb

Total climb adds up every uphill section on the route. It answers: “How much climbing is there overall?”

Elevation range

Elevation range is the difference between the highest point and the lowest point on the route. It answers: “How big is the route’s vertical span?”

Highest point and lowest point

These show the top and bottom elevation values found in the route data.

Planned routes

For planned routes, climb numbers are still estimates based on the imported elevation data. They are useful for orientation, but they are not proof of a journey completed.

Quick guide

  • Use Elevation range when you want the vertical spread of the route.
  • Use Total climb when you want all the uphill sections added together.
  • Use Highest point and Lowest point when you want the route’s elevation limits.

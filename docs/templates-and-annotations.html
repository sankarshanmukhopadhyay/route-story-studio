Templates and annotations

Choose a layout that matches the destination of your route story. Portrait, square and landscape remain available, with Editorial Photo, Expedition Log, A4 and Letter print layouts added in this increment.

Add an annotation

  1. Enter a short label such as a summit, camp or stop.
  2. Move the position slider to the approximate location along the route.
  3. Select Add annotation.

Annotations remain attached by their percentage along the route, so they survive layout changes. Up to 500 annotations are allowed per project.

Print exports

A4 and Letter layouts are designed for standard-resolution print export. High-resolution 2× export may be unavailable when it would exceed the pixel safety budget.

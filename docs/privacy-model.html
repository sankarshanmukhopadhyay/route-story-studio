Privacy model

ActionProcessing
GPX/KML import, projects, photos and exportLocal browser
Map-link reviewLocal browser
Place resolution and routingSelected routing provider after explicit action
Map backgroundOpenStreetMap tiles after explicit consent

Credentials

Provider keys remain in browser session storage unless “Remember key” is selected. They are excluded from projects, generated routes and exports, and can be cleared from the provider panel.

Minimise disclosure

Use coordinates to avoid geocoding, and use solid or photo backgrounds to avoid tile requests.

ROUTE STORY STUDIO v0.3.0

Known limitations

  • Route geometry cannot be edited directly.
  • OpenStreetMap's standard tile service is best-effort and may be unavailable.
  • Offline map packages and animation are not supported.
  • The advanced Google Maps workflow requires a separately deployed resolver and a user-supplied openrouteservice key.
  • Short-link expansion and provider routing may fail because of external service, CORS, quota or URL-format changes.
  • Reconstructed provider routes may differ from the route originally shown by Google Maps.

Elevation values

The poster uses elevation range for a simple headline number. The route summary also shows Total climb, which adds up every uphill section and may be more sensitive to noisy or terrain-derived elevation data.

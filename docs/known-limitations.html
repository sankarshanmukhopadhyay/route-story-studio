Known limitations

  • Google Maps short links are recognised but not expanded in the static browser application.
  • The initial routing adapter requires the user's own openrouteservice API key.
  • Provider-generated geometry may differ from Google Maps.
  • Traffic, manually dragged paths and hidden Google routing preferences cannot be reproduced reliably.
  • Generated routes are planned routes, not evidence of travel.
  • Closer map zoom may crop route ends.
  • No automatic fallback to another routing or tile provider occurs.
  • Route geometry editing and turn-by-turn navigation are not included.

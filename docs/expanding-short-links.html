Expand a Google Maps short link

Two separate services are involved. The resolver expands the approved Google redirect. openrouteservice resolves places and generates planned-route geometry later.

User sequence

  1. Paste a maps.app.goo.gl link.
  2. Select Review route intent.
  3. Enter the deployed resolver endpoint.
  4. Select Expand short link and review.
  5. Review the recovered origin, destination, waypoints and mode.
  6. Continue to openrouteservice only after confirming the intent.

What the resolver may do

It follows at most five redirects across an exact Google Maps host allowlist and returns the final URL plus minimal redirect metadata. It does not scrape Google Maps, geocode, route, or retain journey content.

Evidence

The later route-generation receipt records the final host, redirect count, resolution time and a SHA-256 hash of the original short URL. Full source URLs are not copied into the receipt.

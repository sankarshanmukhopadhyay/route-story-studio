Consent, load and zoom a map background

  1. Select Map background.
  2. Choose the framing: wide, fit, closer or closest.
  3. Press Consent and load map now.
  4. Wait for valid tiles and confirm attribution appears.
  5. Use − or + to reload a different zoom after consent.

Closer views may crop route ends. This is shown in the zoom label and load status. Fit whole route is the default.

Provider policy

Map requests use the official OpenStreetMap tile endpoint, normal browser caching, a valid origin Referer, at most nine tiles, two concurrent requests and short pacing. HTTP 403 and known blocked-tile imagery are rejected before export.

Privacy

Map tiles disclose the visible route area and normal network metadata. Solid and photo backgrounds remain local.

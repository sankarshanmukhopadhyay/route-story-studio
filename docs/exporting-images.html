Route Story Studio · v0.3.0

Exporting images

The exported poster displays distance, elevation range and, when available, duration.

FormatBest for
SVGScalable publication artwork and later design work
PNGGeneral sharing, social posts and transparent-capable output
JPEGSmaller photo-led images

Map-backed exports

Export stays disabled while map mode is selected but no valid map has loaded. HTTP 403 responses and recognised blocked-tile images are rejected rather than embedded. OpenStreetMap attribution remains visible in every applicable export.

Poster statistics

Elevation range means the highest point minus the lowest point represented in the route. If you need more detail, the route summary also shows Total climb, Highest point and Lowest point.

Resolution limits

Standard and high-resolution options are bounded by the export pixel budget. Print layouts may reject a 2× export when it exceeds that safety boundary.

Metadata

Exports are newly rendered in the browser. Original photograph metadata and route-file contents are not copied into the image.

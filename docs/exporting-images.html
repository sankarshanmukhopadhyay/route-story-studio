Exporting images

Route Story Studio exports the same poster design in two formats.

FormatBest for
SVGScalable editing, print and high-quality reuse
PNG standardMessaging, web pages and quick sharing
PNG high resolution (2×)Larger displays and higher-density output

Output dimensions

LayoutStandard PNG2× PNG
Portrait1080 × 13502160 × 2700
Square1080 × 10802160 × 2160
Landscape1600 × 9003200 × 1800

The original GPX is not embedded in exported images. Filenames are derived from your title and cleaned before download.

SVG is the canonical rendering format. PNG is created locally by rasterising that SVG in your browser.

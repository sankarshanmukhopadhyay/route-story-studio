      What do the elevation terms mean?

      Elevation gain is cumulative climbing across the route.

      Elevation range is highest point minus lowest point. It describes vertical span, not total climbing effort.

      Net elevation change is finish elevation minus start elevation. It may be small even when a route contains substantial climbing.

      Read the elevation metrics guide

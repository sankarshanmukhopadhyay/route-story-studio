# Project Governance

The repository owner acts as maintainer and release authority during the incubation phase.

## Decision scope

- Maintainers approve changes to supported formats, privacy boundaries and routing providers.
- Contributors may propose templates, parsers and documentation through pull requests.
- External processing must not be introduced implicitly.

## Enforcement and revocation

Changes that violate the documented privacy boundary may be reverted. Provider integrations may be disabled when their terms, security posture or data-handling behaviour no longer meet project requirements.

## Auditability

Material architectural decisions should be recorded in documentation or an Architecture Decision Record before release.
